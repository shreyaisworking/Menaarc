Our Story in Time

Our Journey

From a small subcontracting team in 2026 to a full-service design and project management consultancy serving global brands across the UAE.

2019

The Beginning

Menaarc began as a focused subcontracting operation, undertaking structural and MEP works on behalf of other consultants and contractors. This foundational period built the technical rigour and site experience that underpins everything we do today.

From these early projects, our founders identified the gap between design ambition and technical delivery and the opportunity to bridge it.

SubcontractingMEP WorksUAE
2020

Joinery Factory

A defining year: Menaarc established its own joinery factory, bringing bespoke furniture and millwork production in-house. This vertical integration gave us direct quality control and eliminated the dependency on third-party workshops.

The factory quickly became a competitive advantage. Enabling faster delivery, higher quality, and custom solutions that off-the-shelf suppliers couldn't match.

Joinery FactoryFurniture ProductionQuality Control
2023

Parent Company

2023 marked Menaarc's formal incorporation as a parent company. Consolidating its various service offerings under a single, structured entity. This enabled us to take on larger and more complex contracts with the right governance in place.

With the Nash project completed and a growing roster of retail and F&B clients, Menaarc's reputation as a trusted consultancy was firmly established.

Parent CompanyNash ProjectCorporate Structure
2024

Custom Furniture Production

Expanding on the joinery capability established in 2020, Menaarc launched dedicated custom furniture production in 2024, enabling full bespoke fitout delivery from concept to manufactured piece.

Major projects this year included the Rosa's Thai fit-out and continued expansion of the Popeyes portfolio, demonstrating our ability to deliver across both high-street retail and quick-service restaurant formats.

Furniture ManufacturingRosa's ThaiPopeyes
2025

Design & PM Division

The launch of a dedicated Design & Project Management division represented Menaarc's evolution into a full-spectrum design consultancy. With its own design team, technical architects, and PMs, Menaarc can now lead projects end-to-end under one roof.

2025 projects include Sushi Art and the Sacoor Brothers multi-location rollout, cementing Menaarc as the go-to partner for global brands entering the UAE market.
