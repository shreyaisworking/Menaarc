About Menaarc

Our Story

A consultancy built on the belief that great design and rigorous execution are never in conflict.

Founded 2026

Built on Expertise
Delivered with Precision

Founded in 2026, Menaarc brings together strong design expertise and in-depth knowledge of developer and authority guidelines to deliver well-planned, compliant, and impactful spaces.

Combining strong design expertise with a deep understanding of mall fit-out standards and submission requirements, our team provides a seamless approach that integrates concept design, technical documentation, authority submissions, and project management, ensuring every project is carefully developed and aligned with developer regulations.

With a focus on commercial projects, Menaarc has quickly risen to become one of the prominent names in interior design across the UAE. With expansion on the horizon, we are set to establish a presence in Saudi Arabia and India.
