What We Do

Our Services

Six disciplines. One seamless process. From creative vision to authority-approved, site-ready delivery.

01

Design & Development

Creative design ideas, space planning, and project vision development for commercial, retail, and F&B spaces. We translate brand identity into spatial experiences that engage, convert, and endure.

Our design process begins with a thorough understanding of your brand, audience, and operational requirements — resulting in concepts that are beautiful, functional, and commercially optimised.

  • Concept Design & Mood Boards
  • Space Planning & Layout Optimisation
  • 3D Visualisation & Renders
  • Material & Finish Specifications
  • Brand Alignment & Creative Direction
02

Project Management

End-to-end project supervision and coordination from concept approval through to final site handover. Our PMs ensure timelines are met, budgets are protected, and quality is uncompromised.

We act as your single point of accountability — coordinating contractors, consultants, suppliers, and authorities to keep your project on track at every stage.

  • Programme & Timeline Management
  • Contractor Procurement & Tendering
  • Site Supervision & Quality Control
  • Cost Management & Variation Control
  • Handover & Defects Management
03

Technical Design

Detailed construction drawings including FF&E drawings, built contractor-ready and compliant with UAE developer standards. Our technical documentation bridges the gap between concept and construction.

We produce the full set of drawings required for contractor pricing, authority submission, and site execution — minimising RFIs and costly design changes during construction.

  • Architectural Construction Drawings
  • FF&E Drawings & Schedules
  • Shop Drawing Review & Coordination
  • Developer Fit-Out Manual Compliance
  • As-Built Documentation
04

Authority Approvals

Managing permits and approvals required from relevant UAE authorities and mall developers. We navigate the complex approval landscape so your project progresses without unnecessary delays.

With deep-rooted knowledge of DM, DDA, Civil Defence, and major mall developer requirements, our team prepares, submits, and follows up on all necessary permits and NOCs.

  • Dubai Municipality (DM) Submissions
  • Civil Defence NOC & Approvals
  • Mall Developer Fit-Out Approvals
  • Trade Licence & Permits Coordination
  • Completion Certificates
05

MEP Design

Design and coordination of mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems for fit-out projects. Our in-house MEP team ensures all services are fully coordinated with the architectural design from day one.

By integrating MEP design in-house, we eliminate the coordination gaps that cause delays and cost overruns — delivering a unified, buildable package from the outset.

  • HVAC Design & Coordination
  • Electrical Layout & Load Calculations
  • Plumbing & Drainage Design
  • Fire Suppression & Alarm Coordination
  • MEP Authority Submission Drawings
06

BOQ & Estimation

Preparation of detailed quantity take-offs and cost estimates for accurate budget planning. Our BOQ documentation provides the clarity needed for competitive tendering and budget sign-off.

We produce itemised bills of quantities aligned with the design intent and market rates — giving you a realistic cost picture before contractor engagement.

  • Detailed Bill of Quantities
  • Cost Planning & Budget Reports
  • Tender Documentation & Analysis
  • Value Engineering Recommendations
  • Post-Contract Cost Monitoring
